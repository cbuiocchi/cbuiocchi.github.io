Air Ride Prototype

Air Ride is a vehicle prototype inspired by the machines in Kirby Air Riders, one of my favorite games of all time. The vehicle was technically fast but didn't feel fast, so this case study is about adding juice to sell the speed. I recolored and retextured the ground so motion reads clearly, added camera shake on boost, speed lines that scale with velocity, and sound effects, and the vehicle now feels much faster without its actual speed changing at all.
