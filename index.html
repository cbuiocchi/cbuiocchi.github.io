Deckborn is an RPG with deckbuilding combat — a bigger-scope take on the genre inspired by classic JRPGs like Final Fantasy 7. It's a solo project built in Unreal Engine 5, with a basic turn-based card combat system and a small overworld to explore. The vertical slice released on itch.io on August 31, 2026.

Entry 14

Card Art and Set Dressing

August 27, 2026

Card art

  • All 22 cards have art. The art system had been in the code the whole time drawing every card image at zero opacity, so the pictures were rendering invisibly.

Set dressing

  • 96 props through the valley: rocks, ferns, flowers, stumps, fallen logs, mushrooms. No new trees, the tree layer was already dense, what was missing was everything at ground level.
  • 62 more in the enemy camp, which had been two towers and a campfire on a bare lawn. Crates, barrels, a mess area, bedrolls, a cart. Anything shorter than knee height got cut, it just reads as clutter from this camera height.

Sound

  • Hover and click sounds on every button in the game.

Card hover

  • Hovering a card lifts it, straightens it, and draws it on top of its neighbors. It used to slide toward the middle of the fan and stay buried under every card to its right.

UI animations

  • Nine animations. Bars slide toward their new value instead of snapping, the turn banner takes the color of whose turn it is, the player box flashes when you get hit.

The Warlord's new plan

  • Cut the Card of Power. Instead he's trying to drown the valley in eternal darkness.

Keyword tooltips

  • Hover a keyword to find out what it does. Works on cards in your hand, cards in the shop, cards in the deck menu, enemy intent lines, and your own status readout.
  • Frail, Weak, and Vulnerable are three different penalties and nothing in the game had ever explained any of them.

Potion slots

  • Three color coded slots along the bottom of the player box, instead of text lines floating over the left of the screen.

Shop header

  • Shows a real health bar and a gold chip instead of one long packed line of text. The Remove card button turns into Back while you're picking the card to remove.

Hero death

  • The hero used to freeze mid-idle and get covered by the defeat screen on the same frame they died. They fall now, and the screen waits for it.

Also

  • The gold readout is back in exploration, a later rewrite had quietly dropped the line that created it. Victory and defeat screens animate in. Enemy panels restyled to match everything else. The Lizard Warrior stopped stabbing the Bishop Knight in the back of the head.
Entry 13

Color Identity and the Waterfall Cave

August 24, 2026

Color identity

  • Royal purple, gold outline, rounded corners, applied to buttons, dialogue boxes, drop slots, health bars, and all four title screens.
  • Title uses royal purple Firlest font with gold outline.

Dungeon enemies

  • Faster, and they notice you from further off. A player can no longer outrun a dungeon enemy.

Waterfall cave

  • There's a cave behind the valley waterfall with a chest in it containing 100 gold.

Pause during shop

  • Pause used to silently do nothing while the shop was open. It works now, and the shop says the clock is paused, since the countdown hides itself inside menus and there was no way to tell it wasn't burning.

Hit weight

  • Impacts scale with damage. A 2 damage chip and a 20 damage hit used to produce the identical flash, punch, and screen shake.

Also

  • Enemy weapons sit in their hands correctly now, five of the nine armed enemies were gripping wrong.
Entry 12

Cards Queue Up

August 21, 2026

Cards queue

  • Dropping a card no longer locks you out until the swing finishes. Play three in a row and they resolve back to back in the order you dropped them.
  • The hand also used to tuck itself off screen while a card resolved, which hid it during the exact window you'd want to read it. It stays up for the whole player turn now.

Card snap

  • Cards now "snap" into their target slots.

Shop layout

  • Redesigned shop menu UI.

Reflection

The card queue is one of the best changes I've implemented so far. The combat was feeling a bit too slow before, but now that players can chain actions, combat can resolve a lot faster. The card "snap" feature is also a small but engaging UX touch.

Entry 11

First Build, First Playtest

August 18, 2026

First build, first outside playtest

  • Packaged the game for the first time and ran my first playtest!
  • Playtest notes: camera is inverted and too sensitive, energy and gold are hard to find, the dungeon sign is too dark to read, encounter three is too hard.

Impact numbers

  • Numbers pop off whatever got hit, and they show what actually landed after Strength, Vulnerable, and Block.
  • Poison ticks flinch the target, throw a green number, and hold a beat before the turn moves on.

Card text is accurate

  • A card in hand shows what it will actually do. With 3 Strength, Spirit Slash reads "Deal 9 damage" in green. Frail drags your block cards down and tints them red.

Enemies have weapons

  • Every armed enemy in the game had been fighting bare handed since the monster packs went in. The weapons ship as separate pieces and nothing was ever attaching them.

Also

  • The Bomb gets lobbed in an arc instead of exploding out of your hand. The Warlord now summons a skeleton and an evil mage to aid him in battle. End Turn and the potions hide during the enemy's turn.

Reflection

Gameplay-wise, the first playtest was a major success! Most of the changes that need to be made involve balancing the dungeon encounters and iterating on the UI.

Entry 10

A Second Chance

August 14, 2026

Playthrough fixes

  • Enemies killed by poison still got their attack off. The death check was running before poison damage landed.

Telegraphs say everything

  • "Next: ATK 4 + Poison 3" instead of "Next: ATK 4," which had been hiding the poison entirely.

Dungeon layout

  • Two chambers, the fork, a third chamber, then the boss.
  • Taking one branch of the fork now seals the other permanently, at both ends. You could previously clear the rat den, walk backwards up the other corridor, and drink the fountain too.

Second chance

  • Die in the dungeon and you wake up outside the shop with one minute on the clock and the dungeon repopulated behind you. Die a second time and the run is over.

Cards

  • Inspiration: discard a card to gain energy. The first card that costs you something other than energy.
  • Shattering Wall reworked to "lose all your block, deal that much damage to every enemy." It was a rare card that did nothing on turn one and built a wall despite being named for shattering one.

Also

  • Reworked potion buttons in shop.

Reflection

I wanted a way for players to get a second chance without allowing them to get too strong. The player has 1 minute to use the gold they gained from the dungeon so far in order to rework their deck. After that, if they lose, it has more to do with the decisions the player made rather than the game itself.

Entry 09

The Return Transition Bug

August 12, 2026

Return transition

  • Coming back from a fight, the hero used to pop in a half second late, fully solid, in one frame.
  • Cause: the transition system blocks input, and hiding the player was a side effect of that block.
  • Fix: turned off the input block. You can move during the wipe now.

New transitions

  • Diamond wipe into a fight, iris wipe back out, both retimed onto an ease curve.
  • Loading screen covers the roughly 600ms mid-load gap where no UI exists.

Pause menu

  • Escape or P, works in exploration and combat.
  • Fixed: pausing didn't pause the interface, so the valley clock and potions ran straight through it.
Entry 08

The Warlord Fights Back

August 10, 2026

Warlord AI

  • Created a 3 beat combat cycle.

Warlord entrance

  • Warlord now has an evil monologue when you reach him.

Sealed doors

  • Dungeon doors seal behind the player. No backtracking allowed!

Also

  • Sign posted at the fork. AoE cards now play their impact effect on every target hit, not just one.
Entry 07

Music and Combat Sound

August 9, 2026

Music

  • Music loops in all levels.
  • Victory/defeat music fades at 10 seconds instead of playing out, since the valley spawn cuts them off anyway.

Combat sound

  • Cast sound on the cast frame, impact sound on the contact frame.

Reflection

The addition of sound is really bringing everything together!

Entry 06

Health Bars and Damage Timing

August 5, 2026

Health bars

  • Moved off-screen bars onto the enemies, anchored to each creature's height.
  • Same fix applied to the drag-targets, which had the same problem and were hard to hit on short enemies.

Damage timing

  • Damage numbers now pop on hit, and card damage lands on the contact frame instead of instantly on release.

Also

  • Post-fight victory/defeat screen. Tutorial fight (5 non-blocking tips, replays every run). One-use healing fountain at the dungeon entrance.

Reflection

I decided to add a simple tutorial to the game in which the player fights a slime using their starter deck after leaving the base camp. I didn't want to assume the player has played games like Slay the Spire. The Jack dialogue and timer are after the tutorial.

Entry 05

Base Camp and the Orc Nerf

July 30, 2026

Base camp

  • Valley now opens on a camp (fire, tents, canopy, banner gate) instead of an empty field.
  • Fenced path leads to hill with NPC.

Jack / run clock

  • Press E to talk to Jack, finishing his dialogue is what starts the run clock.

Menus

  • Main menu's Play button had never actually been wired to anything.
  • Victory/defeat screens.

Orc nerf

  • Waaaayyy too strong.
  • His rage was ramping up his damage way too fast, so attacks and buffs are more spaced out.

Also

  • Roaming enemies no longer wander into the shop yard.

Reflection

I added an NPC with dialogue that explains the player's goal of defeating the Warlord before triggering the timer.

Entry 04

Fixing Combat Timing

July 26, 2026

Hero animation

  • Each card has its own player animation.

Timing fix

  • Damage, hit flash, screen punch, flinch, and death were all firing on the attack's first frame, so the hero swung at a corpse.
  • Everything's scheduled off real animation length now, including the Aura Strike projectile, which arrives on the damage frame.

Run clock

  • Added a countdown to the valley. No timer = infinite money.

Dungeon fork

  • Two paths in the dungeon: left is an elite rat encounter with a rare card drop, right is a healing fountain. Taking one closes the other.

Reflection

I made the difficult decision to add a timer to the game, even though doing so essentially makes my game a roguelike (which I've been avoiding). However, I believe that if there is no timer, the game will have no balance and the player could get bored. I think a timer is the appropriate choice for a vertical slice.

Entry 03

Dungeon, Cards, and AI

July 25, 2026

Enemy AI

  • Each enemy now picks from weighted moves, favors defense when hurt, won't repeat a move twice in a row.
  • In the valley they notice, chase, and give up and return home if you outrun them.

Combat camera

  • Cut camera swoops in favor of a stationary camera (the camera movements were unnecessary and nauseating).

Cards

  • Designed 20 cards, some of which introduce new mechanics such as poison and strength.

Shop

  • Gold from fights buys new cards, removes unwanted ones, or heals you.

Dungeon

  • Designed dungeon level.
  • Built as a 6-enemy gauntlet into the Warlord.

Reflection

The cards have been made and combat is running very smoothly! I also have three different starter enemies: a slime, a spider, and a spiky turtle thing. Each has their own individual move set. They spawn adjacent to the shop, so the player is encouraged to test out their build on weaker enemies. I'm planning on adding an enemy camp across the river that has harder enemies that are worth more gold.

Entry 02

Cutting the Village Hub

July 17, 2026

Village hub cut

  • Cut the Village/Town hub as its own zone. Its four prep locations (heal, buy, remove) are consolidated into a single Shop, now a stop inside the Valley instead of a separate space.
  • World is two zones now instead of three: Valley (exploration + Shop) and Dungeon.

Training Grounds cut

  • Cut the free practice fight (no gold cost, no real HP or death risk) permanently, not just for the current slice. Players are encouraged to test their builds on the weaker enemies near the shop instead.

Reflection

Originally Deckborn was gonna have three main levels: The Valley, the Dungeon, and the Village. The village would've acted as a player hub, but the scope was just too big. Cutting a whole zone gives me much more time to polish the most engaging parts of the game: exploration and combat.

Entry 01

First Playable Fight

July 6, 2026

World

  • Valley and combat levels designed using the terrain tool.
  • Wandering into an enemy pulls you straight into a fight.

Scope

  • Cut the card crafting system in favor of a simpler gold-based progression system.

Combat core

  • Built and tested headless first, so I could confirm the rules worked before there was anything to look at.
  • Built the card system using UMG and Blueprints.
  • Dragging a card to resolve a fight now works!

Reflection

I was able to create the foundation for what will become Deckborn. The player can walk around in one level, and when they come into contact with an enemy, they get transported to the combat level. I've only designed a basic attack so far and tested it in order to progress through combat. The enemy is just a punching bag at this point in production, but it will soon have ways to respond to the player.
